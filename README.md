# Bog Road Walk

Offline phone map of 844 Bog Road (Hillsborough, NH) for walking the proposed easement lines and dropping
"easement OK" / "keep for us" pins with geotagged photos. Pins stay on the phone; "Share all pins" makes a .zip
(bogwalk.json + pins.kml + pins.geojson + photos) to AirDrop to the other phone or a Mac.

Rebuild tiles and layers: `844 Bog Road Maps/_src/lots/build_walk_app.py`. Bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` after any change.
Imagery: USGS/USDA NAIP (public domain). Lines are from the town tax map and draft sketches, not a survey.
